Core Management Capabilities

Every tool required to manage personal and household finances in India, organized into structured modules.

Income & Cash Flow Tracking

Log salary, freelance invoices, business dividends, and rental inflows. Filter records by month and monitor your net cash inflow with zero rounding errors.

Categorized Expense Ledger

Organize daily transactions across 9 standard categories: Food, Rent, Transport, Education, Healthcare, Entertainment, Shopping, Utilities, and Other.

Monthly Category Budgeting

Define realistic spending ceilings for individual categories. Track real-time progress bars and receive visual warnings when spending exceeds 80% or 100% of limits.

Tangible Savings Goals

Define target funds for Emergency Reserves, Tech Hardware, Education, or Travel. Record deposit increments and track deadline completion milestones.

Gemini AI Financial Advisor

Runs structured analysis of your actual month-to-date spending. Returns objective summaries, high-burn alerts, budget recalibrations, and practical weekly checklists.

Private & Self-Contained

All records reside in your local SQLite database file. No external telemetry, no selling of user profiles, and no automated bank account harvesting.

How It Works

A straightforward four-step workflow to maintain complete control over your cash flow.

1

Record Monthly Inflows

Enter your primary salary, freelancing earnings, or investment returns to establish your baseline revenue in Indian Rupees.

2

Log Categorized Expenses

Record purchases and bill payments daily. Assign items to predefined categories to prevent unmonitored cash leaks.

3

Enforce Category Budgets

Establish spending caps per category. The dashboard compares actual expenditures against limits and flags warning thresholds.

4

Review AI Insights

Request Gemini AI reviews to discover high-burn categories, recalibrate budgets, and track progress toward your savings goals.

Engineered for Academic & Production Integrity

Clean separation of concerns with modern Python backend, relational data models, and zero frontend state contamination.

Backend Architecture

  • Python 3.11+ with Flask REST framework
  • SQLAlchemy ORM with strict foreign keys
  • Session authentication with hashed passwords (pbkdf2:sha256)
  • All mathematical aggregates computed server-side

AI Integration

  • Official Google GenAI SDK integration
  • Structured prompt templates enforcing strict JSON return schema
  • Zero hardcoded keys; strictly loaded from root .env
  • Deterministic fallback calculations if offline or rate-limited

Frontend Architecture

  • Semantic HTML5 and modular CSS3 architecture
  • Pure Vanilla JavaScript with asynchronous Fetch API calls
  • Chart.js dynamic visual rendering for distributions and trends
  • Responsive layouts tailored for desktop and mobile screens
